Welcome to TonCry

TonCry is an entertainment game — a mining simulator. Servers are virtual, no real mining takes place.

TON CRY are in-game coins. They are not a cryptocurrency and cannot be exchanged for money or TON. Weekly rewards come from a limited prize fund and are not guaranteed.
